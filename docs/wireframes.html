SewaSync — Wireframes

Low-fidelity layout specification for the core five tabs and their key flows. Every screen below is traced from the shipped implementation in components/*Screen.tsx, so section order, labels and control types reflect what actually ships — not an aspiration. Grey blocks stand in for imagery and copy weight; annotations in ○ call out behaviour that is not obvious from the box drawing.

Stack Next.js 15 App Router · React 19 · Tailwind 3.4 · Plus Jakarta Sans Target 390 × 844 (mobile-first, phone-framed) Nav 5-tab bottom bar, no desktop chrome Demo fixture Rumah Kalantan 2 · Aiman Hakimi · pinned to 15 Sep 2026, 09:41

1 — Notation

Copy lineGrey bar = a run of text. Height tracks the type scale (micro → hero).
CardWhite, 1px border, 16px radius. The default content container.
Hero / tinted blockThe dark hero on Home, or a tinted notice. Drawn dark here.
PaidStatus pillTeal = brand/selected, green = paid, amber = part-paid, red = overdue, grey = neutral.
NextButtonFilled = primary action. Outline = secondary. Red outline = destructive.
InputBoxed field. Dashed/empty = placeholder text present.
ABSegmentedSingle-choice row or tab strip. Active segment carries a teal underline.
Photo slotHatched area for a camera capture or a supplied image.

A box drawn with a teal outline is in its selected/active state on the screen shown. Amounts are the real seeded figures, so the arithmetic on screen agrees across all ten screens — RM 412.80 of TNB splits to 95.46 / 95.46 / 134.97 / 86.91, and Aiman owes RM 41.40 across two bills.

2 — Screen index

#RouteScreenJob to be done
01/HomeAnswer "what do I owe, and is my house okay?" in one scroll, without navigating.
02/bills/BillsScan and filter the ledger; drill into any bill for the working.
03/repairs/RepairsTrack what is broken, who reported it, and how far it has moved.
04/house/HouseAdminister rooms, meters, people and house settings; read the activity log.
05/account/AccountProfile, subscription, notification and language preferences, support, sign-out.
06/bills/new/Add a bill — 1 of 3Capture what the bill is for.
07/bills/new/Add a bill — 2 of 3Choose how it splits; per-method inputs appear inline.
08/bills/new/Add a bill — 3 of 3Review the live allocation and send, or save a draft.
09/bills/view/?id=Bill detailDefend a number: per-person breakdown, the derivation, payments made.
10/bills/pay/Record a paymentLog that money moved — full or partial, for yourself or a housemate.
11/repairs/new/Report a repairSubmit a fault with a required photo so the landlord can act.
12/repairs/[id]/Repair detailRead the timeline and advance the ticket's status.

Not drawn here, but part of the product: /reminders/, /landlord/, /setup/, /setup/invite/, /snap/, /demo/. Bill detail and repair detail are each statically prerendered at a concrete id, so every link into them works offline — /bills/b-tnb/ and /repairs/t-window/ are both valid URLs.

7 — Cross-screen invariants

These hold on every screen and are worth stating explicitly, because each one is load-bearing for trust rather than for looks.

InvariantWhere it shows
Every money figure is tabular, and every amount is integer sen formatted as RM 0.00. No floats, ever.All 12 screens
Teal is the only decorative colour. Green, amber and red mean paid / part-paid / overdue and appear nowhere as decoration.All 12 screens
Every status is a pill with a word, never colour alone — colour-blind-safe by construction.Bills, Bill detail, Pay, Repairs, Repair detail, House
The custody disclaimer ("does not move money, records that you did") appears on every surface where a payment is recorded or contemplated.Home, Account, Bill detail, Pay, Add bill 3, statement sheet
Any figure a person may dispute is one tap from its derivation, and the derivation is produced by the same engine as the figure.Home, Bill detail, Add bill 3, statement sheet
Reminders are fixed wording with only the amount varying, and are logged rather than transmitted.Add bill 3, Reminders
Destructive actions are red, outlined, and never the default primary. A bill with recorded payments offers Void, never Delete, so history survives.Account, Bill detail
Bottom tab bar is present on the five primary destinations only, and hidden on wizard and detail screens where it would compete with the primary action.Absent on screens 06–12
Touch targets are 44px minimum; dense rows use the divider list rather than shrinking the target.All 12 screens
All copy resolves through the string table in lib/i18n.ts, so English and Bahasa Malaysia are structurally guaranteed to stay in step.All 12 screens

Note on this document. These are wireframes, not mockups: they fix layout, hierarchy and interaction, and deliberately carry no brand colour, imagery or finished type. Colours are noted in prose only. The real styling lives in tailwind.config.ts and the ss-* / od-* classes in app/globals.css; nothing here needs to change when that design system evolves.
